FAQ

Before you order

How do I place an order?

Send us a message on Instagram with the piece, the size and the colour. We reply with the price and the delivery date.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes. Every piece is made after you order, so the cord colour is yours to choose.

Can you make a piece to a specific size?

Yes. Send us the measurements or a photo of the space and we will suggest the right size.

Can a piece be personalised?

The hair accessory holder can carry a name. Tell us the name and the colours when you order.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the piece and the size. We confirm the date with you before work begins.
